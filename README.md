### 🚀 About Me

I'm a software engineer from **Mymensingh, Bangladesh**, currently studying Computer Science while running two ventures:

- 🏢 **Founder & Lead Developer** at **[Smart Web BD](https://smartwebbd.com)** — building web platforms and client solutions
- 🎮 **Founder** of **[Risha Apps](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5809257546461430324&hl=en)** — an indie mobile studio shipping games and productivity apps to millions on Google Play
- 🎵 Self-taught **music producer**, releasing hip-hop, jazz, house, and chillout instrumentals worldwide
- 📖 Published **author** of *[Micro Mayhem: A Darkly Humorous Tale of Retaliation](https://www.amazon.com/Micro-Mayhem-Darkly-Humorous-Retaliation-ebook/dp/B0C3MR44VC)* — a sci-fi novella blending dark comedy with themes of revenge and survival

I like building things end-to-end — from backend architecture to UI polish to the app store listing.
